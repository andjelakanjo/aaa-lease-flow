PARKING
Parking spaces — vodi se kroz Easy Storage sistem, van je scope-a implementacije novog softvera
Parking — Easy Storage (van scope novog softvera)

AAA Lease rents parking spaces at the Chicago yard through Easy Storage — a separate platform that handles parking independently from lease/rental operations.

How It Works
  • Month-to-month agreements only — no long-term contracts
  • Billed under an individual's name, not the company name.
  • Typical rate: $250/month per space.
  • Easy Storage automatically pulls monthly payments from customers
Tina's Role
  • Monitors payment statuses in Easy Storage daily.
  • If payment fails: calls customer, manually processes retry
  • Creates QB invoices for: parking deposits, late fees, lockout fees, FI fee (Easy Storage processing fee deducted from payout)
  • Includes parking amounts in the daily billing list to Milica.
Key Accounting Notes
  • Parking revenue booked to a separate revenue line.
  • FI fee (Easy Storage system charge) deducted automatically and booked separately in QB.
  • No integration needed with new lease management software
  • Easy Storage remains the permanent system of record for parking.
