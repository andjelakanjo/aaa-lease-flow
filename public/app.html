Process Map

Customer ↔ AAA Lease

👤 CUSTOMER / KLIJENT
🏢 AAA LEASE
① Inquiry & Approval
Contacts AAA Lease. Submits application, company docs, driver's license, DOT number.
→
Brankica
Checks credit score, bank balance, FMCSA status, True Funding link. Sends to Alex.
Waits for decision. If rejected — end. If approved — continues with documentation.
←
Alex
Final approval or rejection. Defines contract terms.
② Contract & Documentation
Signs contract via DocuSign. Pays down payment. Sends COI (Certificate of Insurance).
↔
Andjela T. / Valentina
Prepares contract (DocuSign: salesman→client→Alex→accounting). Verifies COI and down payment. Creates down payment invoice in QBs.
— waits for GPS confirmation and release form —
←
Valentina / Brankica
Checks GPS in FleetLocate/Routemate. When all OK — issues release form.
③ Unit Pickup
Comes to pick up unit. Signs checkout inspection. Receives registration copy and contract.
↔
Fabrizio
Performs checkout inspection, photographs unit condition, sends report to client and accounting.
— —
Andjela T.
Changes unit status to Active in Active Units List. Updates HubSpot. Creates Drive folder.
④ Active Contract
Uses unit. Receives invoices (weekly or monthly). Pays via ACH bank pull (JPM template).
←
Tina
Sends invoices every Monday (rent weekly) and 1st/15th (lease). One by one through QBs Memorized list.
Receives additional invoices: tolls, repairs, registration, mileage overage.
←
Hanna / Brankica
Hanna: tolls, repairs, final charges. Brankica: mileage overage monthly (lease, 130k/yr free).
Payment is auto-pulled from bank (ACH). If rejected — receives call from accounting.
→
Milica Doson
Daily: reviews payments and rejections. Calls clients for rejected pulls. Closes invoices: rent→tolls→lease.
⑤ Issues (optional)
Reports accident. Submits claim form and adjuster info from their insurance.
↔
Aleksandra / Brankica
Receives claim. Tracks insurance (adjuster, settlement). Billing does NOT stop until insurance check arrives.
Late on payments. Receives warning. GPS starter gets disabled if no payment.
←
Milica + Alex
Recovery: warning → GPS disable → Teo picks up unit ($2,500 fee) → demand letter → lawyer. "Lawyer" status = all billing stops.
⑥ End of Contract
Buyout: pays remaining amount + buyout. Receives title. Registers in their name (IL/IN — Secretary of State; others — via title copy).
↔
Milica → Aleksandra → Emily
Milica sends buyout notice + amendment. Aleksandra creates bill of sale, requests bank payoff. Emily coordinates title transfer.
Return: brings unit back. Signs check-in inspection. Receives final billing for mileage overage and damage.
↔
Fabrizio → Andjela T. → Tina
Check-in inspection + damage charges. Status → Returned/Paid Off. Tina: credit memo if overpaid. Unit → Available.
